About Fly High

The story behind
Fly High.

Fly High Flight Services was created from a lifelong connection to aviation and a desire to give more people the opportunity to learn how to fly.

Where It Started

A lifelong love of flying.

Teresa grew up flying with her father, a private pilot. Her first experiences in the air felt magical, and that feeling stayed with her long after. It became part of the inspiration behind Fly High.

She loves the freedom and challenge of aviation and wanted to create opportunities for others to learn. Fly High offers access to a low-wing training aircraft without students needing to own a plane.

As a member of The Ninety-Nines, Teresa values the community, encouragement, and connection that aviation brings.

The Name

A name with meaning.

“Fly High” is a phrase connected to Teresa’s late son, Jared. The name holds a deeply personal meaning for Teresa and her family and is a way of keeping his memory close. Fly High carries that meaning into the school as a quiet tribute to Jared and the lasting inspiration he continues to bring to their lives.

Meet the Plane

Hello,
Baby Blue.

A familiar name for a new adventure.

Originally called “Blue Bird,” the aircraft became “Baby Blue.”

Baby Blue has flown to San Luis Obispo, Santa Ynez, and Santa Rosa, with plenty more flying still ahead.
