Fly High Scholarship

Supporting the next generation.

The Fly High Scholarship was created in memory of Teresa’s late son and carries forward a name that means so much to her and her family. The $1,000 scholarship is open to students in grades 7–12 and is meant to support a young person with an interest in aviation and the opportunity to keep learning, growing, and exploring what’s possible.
