Discovery Flights

Start with a discovery flight.

If you’re curious about learning to fly, a discovery flight is the easiest place to start. You’ll spend time with an instructor, get familiar with the airplane, and see what flight training is actually like.

It’s a simple introduction for first-time flyers and anyone considering working toward a private pilot certificate.

Book a Discovery Flight

Flight Training

Private pilot training.

Fly High offers flight training for students working toward their private pilot certificate.

Training combines time in the airplane with the knowledge, skills, and decision-making needed to become a safe and confident pilot. Students do not need to own an airplane to begin.
